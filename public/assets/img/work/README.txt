Put photos of real Nice Stitch jobs in this folder, then add a matching entry
in src/lib/work.ts so it shows on /gallery.

Only real work belongs here — this page tells customers "we made these".

Shooting tips:
- Natural light, plain background (a table or wall is fine)
- Fill the frame with the garment; get the print or stitching sharp
- A straight-on shot plus one close-up of the detail works well
- Landscape or square crops best; they display at 4:3
